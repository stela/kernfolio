About Kernfolio

What is Kernfolio?

Kernfolio is a portfolio optimization tool for investors who want quantitative allocation advice without handing their financial data to a third party. You define your positions, enter your views on intrinsic values, and Kernfolio computes optimal weights using established financial models.

Optimization Algorithms

  • Black-Litterman — Combines market equilibrium with your personal views on expected returns, weighted by your confidence level (Idzorek method).
  • Max Sharpe Ratio — Finds the portfolio on the efficient frontier with the highest risk-adjusted return.
  • Minimum Variance — Minimizes portfolio volatility regardless of expected returns.
  • Risk Parity — Equalizes risk contribution across all holdings.

Covariance estimation uses Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage for robustness with limited price history.

Multi-Currency

Hold positions denominated in any currency. Kernfolio fetches daily FX rates and converts all prices to your portfolio's base currency before optimization. Discrete allocation handles mixed-currency portfolios seamlessly.

Privacy

Your total portfolio value, share counts, and cash amounts are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage. The server receives only percentage weights and position types — never absolute monetary values. This means Kernfolio cannot determine how much money you have invested, even if its database were compromised.

Technology

Kernfolio is built with Kotlin and Spring Boot on the backend, a Python microservice (PyPortfolioOpt) for numerical optimization, and a lightweight frontend using Thymeleaf, HTMX, Alpine.js, and Chart.js. Market data is sourced from yfinance and FX rates from the Frankfurter API.
