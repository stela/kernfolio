Multi-Currency Support

Hold positions in any currency. Prices and FX rates are automatically converted to your portfolio's base currency for optimization and discrete allocation.

Privacy First

Your total portfolio value, share counts, and cash amounts never leave your browser. The server only sees percentage weights — no absolute values.
